# LAST SHIFT vision

LAST SHIFT is an original Roblox PvPvE tactical extraction horror shooter. Players enter abandoned industrial zones for supplies, contend with rival teams and environmental threats, and decide how long they can afford to stay before extracting. Audio and lighting should help players read danger while leaving room for uncertainty.

- **Combat** favors decisive hits, readable armor interaction, and ammunition tradeoffs. Future weapon variants, calibers, attachments, and magazine management should extend the same server-owned rules.
- **Survival** uses compact medical decisions. Bleeding, limb injuries, bandages, and medkits should create pressure with a small set of clear actions.
- **Raids** tie inventory, loot, equipment, and extraction to a persistent stash. The cost of taking another fight or searching one more room should be felt before the player leaves the zone.
- **Horror** builds through immersive sound, isolated industrial spaces, original mutants and anomalies, and events that intensify as a raid progresses.
- **Accessibility** starts with responsive controls and clear feedback. The debug range gives us a place to validate weapon feel and balance as the game grows.

Milestone 001 is the shooting range. Raid, survival, inventory, persistence, and horror systems remain on the roadmap.
